Skills Matrix

For each skill, rate your comfort level from 1 to 5 — you don't need to be a 5 everywhere. A higher rating means the team can come to you with a question or you could walk someone through it. You can come back and update your answers anytime by picking your name again.

Who sees your scores? Your scores aren't visible to the team. They're only used to:
  1. Find go-to people for questions and advisor queries.
  2. Help you spot the things you want to learn more about.
What each number means
1New Haven't tried it, or only know it by name. This is the best use of learning time.
2Exploring Tried it a few times and know the basics, but still need a guide or a template.
3Practicing Use it independently on real work and can add to conversations about it.
4Go-to Use it regularly, can coach others, and can troubleshoot when it goes wrong. "Ask me" level.
5Specialist Pushes past what the team does today. Builds new approaches others adopt and spots what's coming.
